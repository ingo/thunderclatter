Sessionable support

Sessionable is a conference app for any event that publishes its schedule on Sessionize. Paste the event's Sessionize link and the schedule, speakers and rooms appear. Everything you add stays on your device.

Getting help

Email support@thunderclatter.com with what you were doing, your device and the app version (shown on the connect screen). Screenshots help.

Common questions

  • Which link do I paste? The conference's Sessionize page, or its sessionize.com/api/v2/…/view/All link. Organizers can find it under API / Embedding in Sessionize.
  • The schedule is missing or stale. The app shows what the organizer has published. Pull to refresh while online; ask the organizer if it still looks wrong.
  • Where is my data? Bookmarks, notes, ratings and attachments live on your device only. Forgetting a conference in the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.

Open source

Sessionable is open source and unofficial: it is not made, endorsed or supported by Sessionize. It is a fork of the JetBrains KotlinConf app, under the Apache-2.0 licence. Source and issues: github.com/ingo/sessionize-app.
